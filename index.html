Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chinaura Express ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our food ordering and delivery service, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and phone number when you register.
  • Delivery location: your delivery address and device location, used to show nearby service, calculate delivery, and track your order during delivery.
  • Order information: items you order, order history, and preferences.
  • Payment information: payments are processed securely by our third-party payment provider (Cashfree). We do not store your full card or bank details on our servers.
  • Device and usage data: basic technical information such as device type and app activity, used to keep the App working and secure.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To show accurate delivery location and live order tracking.
  • To process payments through our payment provider.
  • To send order updates and important notifications.
  • To improve, secure, and support the App.

3. Location Data

The App uses your location to provide delivery to your address and to show the live location of your order during delivery. You can control location permissions at any time through your device settings. Disabling location may limit delivery features.

4. Payments

Payments are handled by Cashfree Payments. Your payment details are provided directly to the payment provider and are subject to their privacy policy. We receive only the result of the transaction (success or failure), not your full card details.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Delivery staff — your name, address, and phone number, so your order can be delivered.
  • Payment provider (Cashfree) — to process your payment.
  • Service providers who help us operate the App, under appropriate confidentiality.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data (see Contact below).

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
